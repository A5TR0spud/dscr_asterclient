x y z radius color
-x = left
+x = right
-y = near
+y = far
-z = down
+z = up

left slider:
up: zoom out (full width becomes visible)
down: zoom in (plane is at about -3, the back 2 corners are barely visible)

bottom slider:
left: reveals right (from above, go counterclockwise) up to 360 degrees
right: reveals left (from above, go clockwise) up to 360 degrees

right slider:
down: look up to about 75 deg (about 3 full rows of square of roof is visible)
up: look down to about 75 deg (about 3 full rows of square of floor is visible)
